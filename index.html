Our Story

Sisterhood &
Craftsmanship.

Sorella means sisterhood. Founded by passionate local pastry chefs, our boutique bakery celebrates community, craft, and culinary excellence in the heart of North Kerala.

We blend the time-honored warmth of Malabar's home kitchens with the precision of French pastry technique — creating zero-preservative, handcrafted masterpieces that tell a story in every slice.

Through our Baking Academy at Mottambram, we've empowered over 200 women across Kannur to transform their passion into a profession — one perfectly frosted cake at a time.
